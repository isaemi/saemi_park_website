About Me

I am passionate about Computer Science, Statistics, and Business, which has inspired me to dive into Data Science. I’ve always been fascinated by how algorithms can solve complex problems and make sense of data. Beyond that, I believe coding is about creating solutions that make the world more inclusive. I am particularly interested in developing tools like voice-controlled applications and AI-powered assistive devices to help individuals with disabilities navigate digital spaces more easily.

Outside of my studies, I enjoy staying active by camping and hiking in the summer and skiing during the winter. For indoor activities, I love aerial arts, including pole, hoop, hammock, and straps, as well as gymnastics and calisthenics. I compete in PSO championships whenever I can and dream of one day performing with Cirque du Soleil. Additionally, I’m a rock music enthusiast with over 10 years of experience playing guitar in both classical and electric styles. I love attending music festivals and concerts of all kinds, including classical, rock, and EDM, as I have a deep appreciation for live music.

Traveling has been a huge part of my life for the past ten years. I’ve read 'Around the World in Eighty Days' so many times, and it always inspires me to get out there and explore. One of my most memorable adventures was a three-month journey through Asia. I spent over a month in China and then made my way to Vietnam, Cambodia, and Thailand. The feeling of Angkor Wat is still fresh in my mind. After that, I traveled to Australia, went hang gliding, and then visited New Zealand, where I explored filming locations from The Lord of the Rings—one of my favorite movies! Traveling always gives me so much motivation. I can’t wait to see where my travels will take me next!
